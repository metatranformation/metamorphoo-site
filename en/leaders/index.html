Core of 12 · Recruitment

A core of 12 leaders in every city

One single criterion: to carry the burden of revival and be ready to sacrifice to accomplish it.

The single criterion

Who can become a key leader?

Leaders come from different churches and professional and social spheres. No title is required: only a burden and availability.

Carry the burden of revival

An inner burden, received from God, for the revival of one's city and nation. It is not a position, it is a spiritual weight.

Be ready to sacrifice

Accepting to give one's time, resources and comfort for the fulfilment of the vision, without seeking first place.

Live in fellowship

Maintaining permanent contact with the visionary couple and the other leaders, ready for any divine activation.

Responsibilities

What a Metamorphoo leader carries

  • Organise METAMORPHOO actions in their area, under the guidance of the visionaries
  • Take charge of the organisational needs of every aspect of every action
  • Take part in regular online and in-person meetings, especially before each major action
  • Walk with new souls and guide them to a local church or the movement
  • Mobilise resources, prayer and partners in their area

Do you represent a church, a company, an association or an institution? Your leadership is precious for mobilising your sphere. Apply: we will reply within 7 days.
