Our vision

Holistic transformation for an authentic and lasting revival in the nations

Born in July 2023 out of a retreat with the youth of Chrisco (Goma district), METAMORPHOO is carried by Pastor – Entrepreneur – Worship leader Fidèle BUMBA OBUTU and his wife Clarice BUMBA. The movement is confirmed by prophetic words and fuelled by the vision of the Chrisco work: the awakening of the Church that will impact nations and governments.

Metamorphoo is not a church, but a movement of the Holy Spirit for the nations. It is a missionary platform that can give birth to churches.

Le symbole du mouvement

From caterpillar to butterfly

La métamorphose n’est pas une amélioration : c’est une transformation totale. C’est l’image que Dieu nous donne dans Romains 12:2, et la raison d’être de METAMORPHOO.

Step 1 · The state of sleep

The caterpillar

en cours

Like the caterpillar, a person can crawl, limited by an unrenewed mind: spiritual, moral and intellectual sleep. Metamorphoo begins with an honest diagnosis of that state.

Step 2 · The time of transformation

The chrysalis

In the secret of the chrysalis, everything is rebuilt. Training, laying on of hands, the Word, repentance and sacrifice: the time when God shapes the vessel before using it.

Step 3 · Taking flight and impact

The butterfly

The butterfly no longer crawls: it flies and it pollinates. The transformed believer becomes an agent of revival in family, church, profession and nation.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Is Metamorphoo a church?
No. Metamorphoo is not a church, but a movement of the Holy Spirit for the nations. It is a missionary platform that can give birth to churches. We work in partnership with local churches and guide newcomers without a church home to a local church.
How do I join the movement?
Fill in the “Newcomer” form on the Contact page or write to us on WhatsApp at +243 997 628 592. You will be registered, automatically followed up by our team, then integrated into a core group or guided according to your situation.
Who can become a key leader?
One single criterion: to carry the burden of revival and be ready to sacrifice to accomplish it. Leaders come from different churches and professional spheres, and organise Metamorphoo actions in their area under the guidance of the visionaries.
How does the online training work?
The Metamorphoo Academy offers YouTube video modules to follow at your own pace. Your progress is recorded. At the end of the track you validate your application: you are then called online or in person for a validation interview.
How can I support financially?
By offering, donation, tithe or vow, in cash or in kind, on a one-off, weekly, monthly or yearly basis. Online payments (FlexPaie, GeneraPay) will be activated progressively; in the meantime, Mobile Money, bank transfer and direct handover are available.
Are my gifts used transparently?
Metamorphoo is a non-profit Christian organisation. God remains the primary source of funds. Every gift is allocated to actions, the Triomphes and Oasis of life projects, and is reported to supporting partners.
