Metamorphoo Academy

Train online, then pass the validation interview

A progressive transformation track, freely accessible, preparing believers to become agents of revival.

The journey

Four steps to your activation

1. Registration

You fill in the registration form. You receive a welcome message and access to the modules.

2. Online training

You follow the video lessons at your own pace, on YouTube, wherever you are.

3. Validation

You validate your track: questionnaire, then an online or in-person call for the interview.

4. Integration

You are assigned to a core group, a department or an action according to your calling and skills.

Evaluation & validation

At the end of the module, answer the validation questionnaire. Selected candidates are called online or in person for the final interview.

Validation interview

Final step: the call

Once the videos are complete, you validate your track. Selected candidates are called online or in person for a validation interview with the visionaries' team.

  • Interview online (WhatsApp / Zoom) or in person in Goma
  • Verification of calling, character and availability
  • Integration decision communicated within 7 days
